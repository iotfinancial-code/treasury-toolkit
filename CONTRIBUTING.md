# Contributing

Corrections are welcome. The aim is that every claim here matches a primary source.

## Suggest a correction

1. [Open an issue](../../issues/new).
2. Say which file (or site page) and, for the claims register, the claim ID, for example `UKT-03`.
3. Quote what it says now and what you think it should say.
4. Link the primary source: a regulator, statute, accounting standard, tax authority page, a company's own filing or announcement, or a project's own release page. A news story or tracker alone is not enough, though it is a useful pointer.

Changes are made on the site first and then copied here, so a fix may take a little while to appear.

## Ground rules

- British spelling.
- No price predictions, trading signals or product endorsements.
- No suggestions to use a single-signature wallet for a company treasury.
- Be kind and specific. Please do not post anything private, such as keys, recovery words, account details or personal information, in an issue.
