# Bitcoin treasury policy template

[bitcoin-treasury-policy-template.md](bitcoin-treasury-policy-template.md) is the open text of the IoT Financial treasury policy template, last reviewed 21 October 2026. It is the same wording as the template on the site, in plain Markdown so you can copy it into your own document, track changes to it, or suggest corrections.

What it covers, in eleven sections: purpose, authority, protected reserves, allocation limits, approvals, custody, accounting and records, tax, review, incidents and communications. Topics that differ by country have a matching US clause and UK clause. Keep both if your company operates in both markets, or delete the one that does not apply.

How to use it:

1. Replace every `[bracketed item]`.
2. Use roles, not personal names.
3. Take it to your own accountant, tax adviser and lawyer before the board approves it.

Related pages on the site:

- [Treasury policy guide](https://iotfinancial.net/guides/policy/), including what a policy should contain and how to adopt it.
- [Policy generator](https://iotfinancial.net/guides/policy/#tool): a shorter first draft built in your browser from your own answers, using the same US and UK clauses.
- [Policy template page](https://iotfinancial.net/template/), where the designed, fillable version is available by email.

The designed, fillable PDF is not in this repository. This folder holds the open text only.

The custody section does not offer a single-signature wallet as an option. One device or one person would be a single point of failure for loss, theft or a device flaw.

**Starting draft only.** The template is an illustration, not a recommendation, and it is not financial, investment, tax, accounting, legal or regulatory advice. It must be reviewed, adapted and approved by your own suitably qualified advisers before it is adopted or relied on. The full notice is at the top and bottom of the template.
